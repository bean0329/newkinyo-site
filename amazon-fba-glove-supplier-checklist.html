Amazon FBA Checklist for Gloves & Sponges

FNSKU, poly-bag rules, carton limits, fee tiers and freight timing - what your supplier must get right before cartons leave Shenzhen.

FBA-ready packaging FNSKU labeling Direct-to-FC shipping
Short answer: Soft exfoliating products are FBA-friendly: light, non-fragile, no battery or liquid compliance. The risks are packaging (poly bags over 4 inches need suffocation warnings), carton weight (50 lb / 22.5 kg max), fee-tier dimensions and label accuracy. A supplier running 100% inspection plus FBA-ready prep removes most of these failure modes at the source.

Plan backwards from your reorder point

Production lead time is 3-15 days for stock constructions and 15-25 days for custom printed runs - then add freight. For a custom printed glove programme replenished by sea, total cycle is roughly 45-65 days, so set your reorder trigger at the point where 65 days of sales remain. We flag long-lead risks in quotation conversations rather than discovering them in week six.

Why packaging fails happen

Most FBA receiving problems for soft goods trace to three causes: missing suffocation warnings, carton weight over 50 lb, and box-content mismatches. All three are packing-line issues, which is why we check counts per carton at the same 100% inspection pass that screens stitching and fabric - the prep table above is executed by the same team, not outsourced.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you ship directly to Amazon fulfillment centers?

Yes - we pack FBA-ready cartons with FNSKU labels, suffocation-warning poly bags, master carton marking per your shipment plan, and ship direct to US and EU fulfillment centers.

Do poly bags for gloves need suffocation warnings?

Yes - any poly bag opening larger than 4 inches requires a suffocation warning. Our standard OPP bags carry the warning text printed on the bag.

What is the carton weight limit for FBA?

50 lb (22.5 kg) per carton. We optimise carton counts to stay under the limit and avoid team-lift fees.

How many days from order to Amazon FC?

Stock constructions: 3-15 days production plus freight (5-8 days express, 8-12 air, 30-40 sea). Custom printed: 15-25 days production plus the same freight options.
