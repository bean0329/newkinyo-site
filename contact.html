How to Get a Quote from Newkinyo

Newkinyo is a factory-direct supplier of exfoliating bath gloves, bath mitts, and African net sponges, based in Shenzhen, China. We answer every inquiry within 24 hours on working days. For the fastest quotation, include four details in your message: the product or SKU you are interested in, your target quantity, whether you need custom logo printing or private-label packaging, and your destination market. With these details we can usually return pricing, MOQ, and lead time in a single reply.

What information should I include in my inquiry?

Product name or a link to the item, your target quantity, any customization requirements (logo printing, color, packaging), and your country or destination market. The more specific your request is, the more accurate our first quotation will be.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Stock designs start at 100 pairs for viscose exfoliating gloves and 300 pairs for sponge gloves. Custom printed logo orders have different minimums depending on the pattern - tell us your design and we will confirm the exact MOQ together with the quotation.

Can I get samples before placing an order?

Yes. We can send existing stock samples for quality evaluation first, and produce printed pre-production samples of your own design after the artwork is confirmed.

Do you support OEM, ODM and private label orders?

Yes - small-batch customization is our main business. We print customer logos directly on gloves and mitts with wash-fast inks, offer 14 stock colors plus Pantone matching, and can pack under your own brand. For the difference between the two cooperation models, see our OEM vs ODM private label guide.

How fast do you reply, and what are your working hours?

We reply to every inquiry within 24 hours, and usually much faster during Chinese business hours (GMT+8). You can reach us through the form on this page, WhatsApp, phone, or email - all channels are monitored daily.

Do you ship internationally, and which documents do you provide?

Yes, we ship worldwide by express, air, or sea freight depending on order size, and we provide commercial invoices, packing lists, and certificates of origin as required by your customs process.
