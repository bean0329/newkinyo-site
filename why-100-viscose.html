What Is Viscose Rayon?

Viscose rayon is a regenerated cellulose fibre produced from wood pulp. The fibre is spun into yarn and woven into a textured cloth that becomes supple when wet. This is why viscose has been the traditional fabric for kessa mitts and hammam gloves for generations, and why it remains the material buyers search for under terms such as viscose bath mitt and viscose exfoliating glove.

How the Fibre Works in Water

When soaked in warm water, viscose fibres swell slightly and the weave gains a controlled grip. Dead skin rolls off in soft strips with circular motion — no chemical exfoliant, no microbeads. The same property makes the mitt reusable and easy to rinse clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is viscose a natural fibre?

Viscose rayon is a regenerated cellulose fibre made from wood pulp. It combines a natural origin with consistent, tunable weave texture, which is why it is the classic material for exfoliating bath mitts.

Why not nylon or silicone?

Nylon is cheaper but harsher and less comfortable on wet skin; silicone does not exfoliate through texture the same way. Viscose swells in warm water to create grip while staying soft.

Is 100% viscose biodegradable?

Viscose is derived from cellulose and is biodegradable under appropriate conditions. Our mitts contain no chemical exfoliants — exfoliation is physical, using warm water only.
