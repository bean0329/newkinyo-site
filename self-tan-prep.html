What Is Self Tan Prep?

Self tan prep is the step that decides whether a self-tanning product looks natural or streaky. Dead skin cells sit unevenly on the surface, so colour develops faster on dry patches and slower elsewhere. An exfoliating mitt for self tan removes that top layer physically — no chemical exfoliant required — and gives a clean, even base.

Why Exfoliate Before Applying Self Tanner?

Retailers in the self-tan category increasingly bundle a viscose mitt with tanning lotion as a prep and maintenance tool. The reason is simple: customers who exfoliate before application report more even colour, and customers who keep a mitt at home can extend the life of each tan. That makes exfoliating mitts for self tan a repeat-purchase accessory rather than a one-off item.

How to Use a Viscose Mitt for Self Tan Prep

Step 1 — soften the skin with warm water for a few minutes. Step 2 — work the damp viscose mitt in gentle circular motions over dry areas such as knees, elbows and ankles. Step 3 — rinse and pat dry, then wait 12-24 hours before applying self tanner. Do not exfoliate immediately before tanning, as freshly exfoliated skin can absorb colour too quickly.

Tan Removal: Resetting the Skin Between Applications

As a tan fades, the top layer of coloured skin flakes unevenly. A tan removal mitt lifts that layer and resets the surface for the next application. Because the mitt is reusable and made from 100% viscose rayon, it is positioned by retailers as a sustainable alternative to single-use tan remover wipes and chemical sprays.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is self tan prep?

Self tan prep is the exfoliation step before applying self-tanning lotion. Using a 100% viscose exfoliating mitt removes the layer of dead skin cells on the surface, so the tan develops evenly instead of patching on dry areas.

Can I use an exfoliating mitt for self tan?

Yes. Exfoliating mitts for self tan are one of the most common uses of a viscose bath mitt. Exfoliate 12-24 hours before tanning, not immediately before, to let the skin settle.

What is a tan removal mitt?

A tan removal mitt is the same exfoliating mitt used after the tan has faded. It lifts the uneven, flaking top layer so the skin is ready for the next application — a simple, reusable alternative to chemical tan removers.

How often should I exfoliate between self tan applications?

Most users exfoliate every 5-7 days. Newkinyo viscose mitts are gentle enough for regular use because the fibre softens in warm water rather than scraping the skin.
