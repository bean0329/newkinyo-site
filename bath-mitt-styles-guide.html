What Actually Changes Between Styles

The base material is the same — 100% viscose rayon in 16 x 22 cm, 16-17 g pieces. What changes is the weave density and roughness: coarse weaves lift more dead skin, softer weaves suit frequent use. Newkinyo supplies single-layer 150D for softer mitts and heavier double-layer constructions for coarse scrub mitts, with 14 stock colours and custom Pantone dyeing from 10,000 pcs.

Choosing a Style for Your Market

For Amazon and mainstream bath ranges, start with the soft Korean style or a standard viscose exfoliating glove. For spa, hammam and dead-skin-remover niches, choose the coarse Moroccan or hammam kessa mitt. Many buyers run two or three styles under one brand using shared packaging, which keeps MOQ low and simplifies shipping.
