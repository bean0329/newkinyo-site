Overview

Unlike a viscose glove, they build a rich lather, feel gentle on sensitive skin and are the standard choice for daily shower use in mass retail and subscription programmes. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for sponge gloves?

MOQ is 300 pairs per style. Mixed styles can be combined in one order, and trial quantities below 300 pairs can be discussed for new Amazon or DTC brands.

How much does a sponge glove cost?

Price depends on sponge density, glove size and packaging. Send your target spec and quantity for an exact EXW quotation - typical programs are quoted per specification rather than from a fixed list.

What is the difference between a sponge glove and a viscose glove?

A sponge glove has a foam core laminated to a nylon face: it creates rich lather and is gentler, better for sensitive skin and daily shower use. A viscose glove is twisted fibre with no foam - firmer exfoliation and faster drying.

Can I customise the logo, colour and packaging?

Yes. We support logo printing, woven labels, Pantone-matched nylon, custom sponge density and packaging from OPP bag to retail box or gift set.

How long do samples and bulk production take?

Samples in about 5-7 working days. Bulk production normally 15-20 working days after sample approval, excluding shipping from Shenzhen.

Do you supply Amazon FBA sellers?

Yes - FNSKU labelling, poly-bagging with suffocation warnings, carton dimension control for FBA fees, and direct shipping to US and EU fulfillment centres.
