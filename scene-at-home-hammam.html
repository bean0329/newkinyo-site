Short answer: An at-home hammam uses heat, water and a hammam glove to exfoliate with no chemicals. The core product is a 100% viscose kessa-style mitt, 16 x 22 cm, MOQ 100 pcs, EXW USD 0.32-0.50.

Step 1 — Heat and steam

Run a hot shower or fill the tub to create a warm, steamy space. Several minutes of heat softens the skin and opens pores.

Step 2 — Exfoliate with a Hammam Mitt or Hammam Glove

Wet the glove and work over the body in firm, rhythmic strokes. Unlike sugar scrubs, a viscose hammam mitt exfoliates with water alone — no fragrance, no chemicals.

Step 3 — Rinse and restore

Rinse away dead skin with warm water, finish with a cool rinse and a light body oil. Skin looks radiant and feels soft.
