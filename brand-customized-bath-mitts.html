Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for custom printed bath mitts?

MOQ is 1,000 pcs per design. The 1,000-pc minimum comes from the print setup: full-surface pattern printing needs dedicated screens or transfer paper, so smaller runs are not economical.

Do the printed patterns fade after washing?

No. We use non-toxic, odourless inks with a fixation process that keeps the pattern stable through repeated washing, and the finished product can pass SDS testing required for EU and US retail.

Can you produce my own artwork or a Pantone-matched design?

Yes. Send vector artwork (AI, EPS, PDF) or a high-resolution PNG. We can also develop the pattern from a reference photo, and Pantone colour matching is available on the print.

How much does a custom printed bath mitt cost?

EXW USD 0.52-0.75 per piece at 1,000 pcs, depending on the number of print colours, coverage and packaging. More colours and full-surface coverage move the price toward the upper end.

How long does sampling and production take?

Pre-production sample with your print in 7-10 working days. Bulk production 15-25 working days after sample approval, excluding shipping from Shenzhen.

Is small-batch custom printing possible?

This is our specialty - we are set up for small-batch customised pattern printing rather than only large volume runs. The standard MOQ is 1,000 pcs and lower trial quantities can be discussed.
