How to Use &amp; Care

How to use

  • Wet the glove with warm water
  • Massage in circular motions on elbows, knees, heels
  • Use with body wash for a smoother glide
  • Rinse and hang to dry after each use

Care notes

  • Slight shrink after first soak is normal
  • Keep the OPP bag sealed before use
  • Do not bleach or tumble dry
  • Replace every 8–12 weeks

Custom service

  • Logo print or woven label
  • Header card & barcode
  • Retail gift set with 2–3 pcs
  • FNSKU labelling for FBA

Quality control

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for 100% viscose exfoliating gloves?

MOQ is 100 pcs per colour for stock colours. Custom Pantone colour matching starts at 10,000 pcs because the yarn must be dyed to order.

How much do viscose bath gloves cost?

EXW price is USD 0.32-0.50 per piece at 100 pcs, depending on colour, size and packaging. Larger orders are quoted lower - send your quantity for a tiered price.

What is the size and weight of each glove?

About 16 x 22 cm (+/- 2 cm) and 16-17 g per piece, packed 1 pc per OPP bag. Export carton: 1,000 pcs, 57 x 36 x 55 cm, N.W. 16.85 kg / G.W. 17.85 kg.

Can I print my logo and use my own packaging?

Yes. Logo can be printed on the glove or on a header card, and packaging can be OPP bag, kraft pouch, PVC box or a retail gift set with your barcode.

How long do samples and production take?

Free sample (freight collect) is usually ready within 3-5 working days. Stock-colour orders take 3-15 days; custom colour or logo orders take 15-25 days.

Do the gloves shrink after washing?

Viscose is a twisted yarn, so a slight shrink after the first soak is normal and expected. Keep the outer packaging sealed until use, and the fit will be consistent.
