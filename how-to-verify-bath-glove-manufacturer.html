How to Verify a Bath Glove Manufacturer

Nine checks that separate real factories from traders and resellers - run them before you send any deposit.

9-point checklist Red-flag table Written for first-time importers
Short answer: Verify a supplier with nine checks: video factory walkthrough, business licence scope, sample-before-payment, QC process evidence, production timeline consistency, communication quality, payment terms sanity, traceability of the "50+ clients" style claims, and a small first order. The 9-Point Checklist

#CheckWhat a real factory looks like
1Live video walkthroughWalks the cutting, sewing, packing floor on request, without a script. Traders refuse or show office interiors.
2Business licence scopeRegistered scope includes manufacturing or textile production, not only "trading".
3Free samples before paymentSends stock samples free (you pay courier). Hesitation on samples is a hard red flag.
4QC process evidenceCan describe inspection steps concretely: stitching, seam strength, elasticity, fabric defects - per piece or per AQL.
5Timeline consistencyQuotes sample lead time (3-5 days) and bulk lead time (15-25 days custom) that match across calls and email.
6Communication qualityAnswers spec questions with numbers, not adjectives. Sloppy answers about GSM, weight or dimensions predict sloppy production.
7Payment terms sanityStandard is 30% deposit / 70% against B/L copy or before shipment. Anything demanding 100% upfront deserves scrutiny.
8Claims you can checkClient-count claims come with methodology and an as-of date. Named references under NDA are normal during vetting.
9Small first orderAccepts a 100-500 pc validation order without sulking. A factory confident in quality does not fear small first runs.

Red Flags That Predict a Bad Order

Red flagWhy it matters
Price far below every other quoteUsually lighter fabric weight, skipped inspection or a reseller margin trap that surfaces as defects
"Same as your sample, trust us"Refusal to send a physical pre-production sample of YOUR spec
No batch traceabilityIf a retail defect appears, you cannot contain the affected batch - Amazon account risk
Vague factory photos (stock images)Reverse-image search the photos; generic textile imagery usually means trader
Pressure to skip inspection to save costInspection is your cheapest insurance; a factory that offers to skip it is telling you what it expects to find

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a Chinese bath glove factory before ordering?

Run the 9-point checklist: live video walkthrough, licence scope check, free samples first, concrete QC evidence, consistent timelines, numeric answers, sane payment terms, checkable client claims, and a small validation order.

What payment terms are standard?

30% deposit with order, 70% balance before shipment or against the B/L copy. Treat demands for 100% prepayment as a warning sign.

Should I use a sourcing agent instead?

Agents add margin and can obscure accountability. A factory that passes direct verification - video audit, samples, traceability - removes the main reasons to pay an intermediary.

How big should my first order be?

100-500 pcs is enough to validate stitching, weight and packing quality without meaningful cash risk. Scale on the second cycle.
