What is the difference between an African net sponge and a loofah?

An African net sponge is woven nylon mesh: it stretches to reach the back, dries quickly and resists mould. A natural loofah is plant fibre — stiffer, non-stretching and needs more careful drying. The net sponge suits daily exfoliation and travel; the loofah suits occasional deep scrubbing.

Can I customize the colour, length and packaging?
