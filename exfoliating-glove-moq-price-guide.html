Worked example: a 500-pc trial order

A typical first-time Amazon seller orders 500 stock-colour viscose gloves with a logo heat-transfer (below print MOQ), OPP bags and FBA carton marking. On this profile the unit price lands near the top of the $0.32–0.50 band, total EXW roughly $200–250, and freight for a 500-pc order fits in a small express or air shipment. Scaling the same design to 3,000 pcs usually pulls the unit price down 8–12% and makes sea freight economical.

What MOQ actually buys you

MOQ exists because setup steps - dyeing, cutting, printing plates, QC line changeover - cost the same whether you make 100 or 10,000 pieces. At 100 pcs you pay a setup premium per unit; from 1,000 pcs the setup is amortised and printing becomes affordable; from 10,000 pcs fully custom constructions and colours open up. Start where your cash allows - a 100-pc stock-colour order is a valid first step, not a compromise.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lowest MOQ for exfoliating bath gloves?

MOQ starts at 100 pcs per design for stock-colour viscose gloves and African net sponges, 300 pairs for sponge mitts, and 1,000 pcs for custom printed mitts. New Amazon sellers can discuss trial quantities below standard MOQ.

How much does a custom printed glove cost compared to a stock colour one?

Custom printing adds roughly $0.20-0.25 per piece over the stock-colour band: printed viscose mitts run $0.52-0.75 EXW versus $0.32-0.50 for stock colours, at a 1,000-pc MOQ.

Are the prices on this page FOB or EXW?

Prices are EXW Shenzhen - they exclude freight, export clearance and import duties. Ask for FOB or DDP quotes for your destination port or Amazon warehouse.

Do you offer free samples?

Yes. Blank or stock samples are free of charge; you pay only the courier cost. Printed samples of your own artwork can be arranged before bulk production.
