What Is a Kessa Mitt?

The kessa mitt (also written kessa glove, kesa mitt or kese mitt) is a coarse-wove exfoliating glove traditionally used with warm water and soap in North African and Turkish baths. It lifts dead skin cells physically — no chemical exfoliant needed. For retailers and Amazon sellers, it sits in the fast-growing bath and body exfoliation category under search terms such as kessa mitt, exfoliating bath mitt and dead skin remover mitt.

Material: Why 100% Viscose Matters

Viscose rayon fibres swell slightly in warm water, creating the grip that makes a kessa mitt work. Compared with nylon, 100% viscose is softer against wet skin, more comfortable for repeated use, and fully reusable. How to Source a Kessa Mitt Supplier

When comparing suppliers, confirm four things: the fibre content (ask for 100% viscose, not a nylon blend), the stock colour range and MOQ, whether logo and packaging can be customised at your volume, and the factory's inspection standard.
